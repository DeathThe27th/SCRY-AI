# Research methodology

Each evidence object has a stable ID, source, optional provider URL, published time, retrieval time, category, normalized statement, and freshness status. Primary company and regulator sources take precedence when they describe the same event as secondary coverage.

The research roles separately analyze price behavior and events, then construct the strongest continuation and reversal cases. The editor reconciles them without adding facts. Output distinguishes direct facts, attributed reporting, multi-source inference, and unknowns. Each thesis includes concrete conditions that would weaken it.

The guardrail score is: freshness × 30 points + primary-source share × 25 + source diversity × 20 + evidence quantity × 15 + retrieval integrity × 10, minus up to 20 points for unresolved contradictions. Scores under 40 are weak, 40–74 mixed, and 75+ strong. This score describes research inputs; it is not statistical confidence or a forecast probability.

Traditional equity closes and tokenized observations use different venue contexts. Scry retains both labels and timestamps. A difference from the official close is a comparison, not proof of price discovery or guaranteed convergence.
