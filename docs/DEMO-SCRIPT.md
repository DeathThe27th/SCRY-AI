# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:25 — The problem.** Open `/`. “Tokenized US stocks can keep moving while Wall Street is closed. Scry AI investigates the evidence; the human decides.” Point to the snapshot capture label and session status.

**0:25–0:55 — Explore the move.** Select NVDA in Discover. Toggle Underlying and Tokenized above the chart. Open `E-001` from the Timeline to show a primary source and timestamp.

**0:55–1:35 — Investigate.** Click **Investigate move**. Name the five stages while they advance: gathering, reading, competing explanations, contradictions, and the final brief.

**1:35–2:15 — Inspect the result.** Show the factual move, citation IDs, bull and bear cases, missing peer confirmation, invalidation conditions, and the 62/100 mixed evidence score. Emphasize that limited breadth lowers confidence.

**2:15–2:40 — Human control.** Enter “Wait for the underlying open and peer confirmation,” choose **Watch**, and open the Decision journal. Open the saved brief.

**2:40–3:00 — Handoff.** Click **Trade on Bitget ↗**. Explain that it opens the official marketplace in a new tab and asks the user to search the token symbol when no verified exact link exists. “Scry prepares the research. You review and execute on Bitget.”
