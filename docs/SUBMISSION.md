## Project description

Scry AI is an AI research desk for 24/7 tokenized US-stock markets. It investigates movements across market, company, filing, macro, peer, and on-chain evidence; builds competing bull and bear cases; validates citations; and records the human’s final decision before an external Bitget handoff.
