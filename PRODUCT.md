# Product

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Scry AI presents stock information, source links and competing research cases. Its existing research desk supports recording decisions and opening an external Bitget marketplace.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js application deployed to Vercel. Current prices and evidence are a historical demo fixture, not live feeds. Homepage bookmarks persist in the browser. No orders are placed by Scry.

## Brand Commitments
The user explicitly requested a Polymarket-style landing page in light mode, following rejection of the dark dashboard design. Stock information and news should lead the landing experience.

## Evidence on Hand
`fixtures/market.ts` contains four stocks and primary-source URLs. No live provider credentials are configured. Specific audience demographics are undecided.
